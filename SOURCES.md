This repository's knowledge files and data restate widely published design principles, frameworks, and research findings **in original wording**, with named attribution for factual and scholarly purposes.

## Frameworks & principles referenced

- **Dieter Rams — Ten Principles for Good Design** (as published by Vitsœ), restated in original wording.
- **Jakob Nielsen — 10 Usability Heuristics for Interaction Design**, Nielsen Norman Group: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/ — used per NN/g's published permission ("You may use these heuristics in your own work. Please credit Jakob Nielsen and provide the address for this page.").
- **Universal Design Principles** — Center for Universal Design, NC State University.
- **SUCCESs framework** (Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories) — Chip Heath & Dan Heath, *Made to Stick*.
- **WCAG 2.1 AA** — W3C Web Accessibility Initiative.
- **Vox-ATypI typeface classification** (historical standard; used as the lineage for the typography taxonomy).
- **Distinctive brand assets & mental availability** — Byron Sharp, Jenni Romaniuk (Ehrenberg-Bass Institute), restated concepts.
- **Brand equity model** — David Aaker. **Brand Identity Prism** — Jean-Noël Kapferer. **Brand archetypes** — Jungian archetype system as popularized in brand practice.
- **Design-system principle sets** referenced comparatively as published: Adobe Spectrum, Microsoft Fluent, Material Design (Google), and design principles published by Lyft Design.
- **Identity-practice positioning** (practice-philosophy table): factual characterizations of how Pentagram, Landor, Mucho, Siegel+Gale, Wolff Olins, and COLLINS publicly describe their own practices.
- **Style and trend research** — synthesized from publicly available 2025–26 design publications and verified against multiple sources at time of writing; per-claim source URLs are retained in `docs/superpowers/` research notes.
